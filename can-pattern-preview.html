Brand Style Guide

CAN Background Pattern

The standard Creator Access Network background: teal-on-gray Creator-tool icons at 15% opacity, with white content cards on top. This is how site sections should look on both desktop and mobile.

Usage specs

PropertyValue
Tile assetcan-pattern-teal-on-gray.svg (960×960 master)
Desktop tile sizebackground-size: 1080px 1080px; background-repeat: repeat;
Mobile tile size (≤767px)background-size: 480px 480px;
Icon colorDeep Sea Dive #2A6478 at 15% opacity, ~1px stroke
Ground colorCool Gray Blue #F0F4F8 (baked into the tile)
Content on patternAlways inside a white card (see below)
